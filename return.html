Eligible & ineligible items

Eligible for return

  • Unused accessories in their original packaging.
  • Items bought from KAKA ENTERPRISES — in store, online, or via WhatsApp.
  • Items with proof of purchase — receipt, order confirmation, or WhatsApp order message.
  • Items reported within 30 days of delivery.
  • Wrong item received, or an item that arrived damaged or not as described.

Not eligible for return

  • Items that have been used, worn, or damaged by you.
  • Items without their original packaging, accessories or labels.
  • Items returned more than 30 days after delivery.
  • Items without proof of purchase.
  • Items damaged by accident, liquid, or a non-compatible charger. Pending
  • Items with removed or defaced serial labels. Pending

This list is indicative and awaiting final confirmation. Message us on WhatsApp if you're unsure whether your item qualifies.

Return conditions

For a return to be accepted, all of the following must be true:

  • The item is unused and in the same condition you received it.
  • It's in its original packaging, with all cables, adapters, manuals and labels included.
  • You can show proof of purchase — receipt, order confirmation, or WhatsApp order message.
  • Your return is reported within 30 days of delivery.
  • The item is packed securely for the journey back to us.

Frequently asked questions

What is the return window?

30 days from the day your order is delivered. After that, we can't accept a return for a change of mind.

Can I return an item I've used?

Our return policy covers unused items in their original packaging. If an item is faulty, it's usually handled under warranty instead — tell us what's wrong and we'll point you to the right route.

Do I get a full refund?

Yes — approved returns receive a full refund of the amount you paid for the item. The method we use to send the refund is still being confirmed with our payment partners.

Who pays for return delivery?

Pending This depends on the reason for the return. Ask us before sending anything back and we'll confirm who covers the cost.

Can I exchange instead of getting a refund?

Yes, where stock allows. Choose Exchange as your preferred resolution on the form and tell us the item you'd like instead. Exchange windows and procedures are still being confirmed — see the exchanges section.

How long does a refund take?

Processing times are still being confirmed with our payment partners — see the timelines table. When your return is logged, our team will give you the expected timeframe.

I've lost my receipt — can I still return the item?

For online and WhatsApp orders we can usually look your order up by order number or phone number. For in-store purchases, any proof that links the item to us helps — a bank transfer record, a photo of the receipt, or the order message. Message us and we'll see what we can do.

Can I bring the item to the store instead of posting it?

Yes. Bring the item, your proof of purchase and a short note about why you're returning it to No. 19 St. Michael's Road, Aba, Abia State. We're open Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM.

What happens if the item is damaged on arrival?

Report it as soon as you can with a photo of the damage. Items that arrive damaged or not as described are eligible for a return. We'll confirm the return address and next steps once we've seen the photos.

Still not sure?

Message us with your order number and a photo of the item — we'll tell you whether it qualifies for a return and what happens next.
